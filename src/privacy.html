02 / Transparency, by design

Privacy Policy

Understand what stays on your device, what you choose to share, and where platform services are involved.

1. Scope and information you provide

AIO Finance is the public-facing app and website operator name. The operating country is the United States. This policy describes the current AIO Finance mobile app implementation and this informational website. Features and platform availability may change as the application develops.

The app can store information you enter, including your profile and household details, financial account references, asset and liability amounts, income, expenses, savings plans, insurance references, owners, beneficiaries, notes, planning assumptions, saved scenarios, and financial history. Some fields are optional. The financial tools use this information to calculate and display your financial position and plans.

AIO Finance uses manually entered financial records. It does not connect to banks or import bank transactions automatically. The financial tools do not require account registration or a connection to a developer-operated financial-data server.

Local storage

The financial profile is saved in the app’s local storage on your device. Settings and recovery information may also remain there. “Local” does not mean that every operation is offline: software updates, platform services, email, and any sharing you initiate can use the network.

2. Encryption and access protection

The app encrypts the locally saved financial profile using a 256-bit AES key, with the key kept through the operating system’s protected credential storage. Password-protected manual backups use encryption with a password-derived key. These protections do not make devices, exported files, or the app immune to unauthorized access or loss.

Optional app locking uses the device’s authentication facilities, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or the operating system’s available fallback. AIO Finance does not receive or store your biometric templates. Hide Balances conceals financial amounts in supported app views; it does not erase records or secure a file you have already exported.

Protection depends on a compatible native build, device settings, and operating system behavior. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

3. Backups, restoration, and cloud services

You can create an encrypted manual backup protected by a password you choose, then copy or save it using your own tools. Keep the password separately; the app provides no password recovery service. Restoration validates a backup and asks for confirmation before replacing local records, preserving a local safety copy where the restoration workflow requires it. Older unencrypted backup formats may be accepted only through an explicit warning and confirmation.

Cloud backup is not currently available. Use an encrypted manual backup to keep a recovery copy of your information.

If cloud backup becomes available in a future release, it may involve encrypted snapshots and account and backup metadata handled by Apple iCloud or Google Drive. Those providers operate under their own policies. Cloud snapshots would not be live synchronization. This policy will be updated to describe any cloud features before they become available.

The device or operating system may separately provide backup, account, and diagnostic services under its own settings. We have not verified their complete treatment of this app’s data across all platforms. Do not assume that a device backup replaces an exported recovery copy.

Software updates and local reminders

The app is configured to check for Expo-hosted software updates when it loads. Expo and the network services delivering updates can process connection information, such as IP addresses, and technical update metadata. No financial-profile payload was identified in the update configuration. Optional quarterly review notifications use local scheduling, rather than an app-operated push service, and request notification permission when enabled. Delivery requires compatible native support and device permission.

4. Information you choose to send

When you export or share a report, the selected PDF can contain readable financial or personal information. PDFs are not protected by the app’s backup password. Your chosen email, messaging, printing, file-storage, or other destination controls what happens after delivery. Review a document before sharing it.

The in-app feedback workflow prepares an email to aiofinancesupport@gmail.com. Bug reports can include app version and release, device and operating system information, the current app screen, theme, language, and local time. Feature requests use a smaller app-information summary. Financial records and saved documents are not automatically attached, but information you type or attach can be included. You review and send the message through your own email app.

If you contact support, the mailbox receives your email address, message, and anything you include. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate issues. Email providers process correspondence under their own policies. Avoid sending full account details, passwords, complete backups, or sensitive financial documents.

SDKs, analytics, and diagnostics

The app uses Expo and React Native services for device functions such as protected storage, authentication, software updates, notifications, files, printing, and sharing. It also includes PDF and cryptographic utilities. Google sign-in code is included for a cloud backup option that is not currently enabled. A library being included does not mean its online service is active.

The current app does not include a dedicated advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party crash-reporting integration. Development-only performance logs use operation names and numeric timings. Apple TestFlight, the operating system, Expo, and other platform services may process their own diagnostics or beta feedback under their policies and your settings. These platform services have their own data practices.

Sharing and sale

AIO Finance does not sell your financial records, share them with advertisers, or upload the financial profile to a developer-operated financial-data server. Data can leave your device through the sharing and support actions described above, platform services, or a future cloud feature you explicitly enable. Applicable legal requests may affect information actually held by service providers or in support correspondence.

This website

This site has no analytics scripts, advertising, contact form, embedded third-party content, or application cookies. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. It does not request your financial information. Once hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s Pages documentation and privacy statement. Following an external link or opening email involves that provider’s services.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Records remain in local app storage until changed, deleted, or removed through the app’s controls. Deleting a current record may leave information in prior financial snapshots or recovery copies. Ordinary history has retention rules; protected checkpoints and some recovery information can remain longer.

Complete App Reset in Settings → Backup & Restore requires confirmation and is designed to remove the financial profile, saved plans, history, notes, settings, and local recovery copies, disable cloud backup, and reset app authentication settings. It does not delete previously exported PDFs, copied backup text, saved files, email attachments, or existing cloud backups. Remove those separately from the services and devices where you saved them. Uninstall and operating system behavior, including protected credential storage, can differ by platform.

You can review and change locally stored information in the app, control optional authentication and notification settings, and choose whether to export or share information. Because no developer financial-data server was identified, support cannot retrieve your local financial profile or recover a forgotten backup password.

Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox while a request is handled and afterward as needed for follow-up, security, or applicable obligations. You may ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to support, subject to applicable law and identity verification.

6. Children’s privacy

AIO Finance is not intended for children under 13. Please do not send children’s personal information to support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the support mailbox, contact us so that the issue can be reviewed and the information removed where appropriate.

7. Policy updates and contact

This policy will be updated when data practices or relevant features change. The published policy will show its effective date. Material changes may also be explained in the app’s release information.
