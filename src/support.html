Report a bug or request a feature

Use More → Feedback & Support in the app, or email us directly. For a bug, explain what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it. Include your app version, device model, and operating system version. For an idea, tell us what would help and why.

Keep your records safe

Your financial records are saved locally. Before resetting or reinstalling, create an encrypted manual backup when possible and store its password separately. Never email passwords or complete backups, and remove financial details from screenshots.
